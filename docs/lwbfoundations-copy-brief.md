## Part 7. Voice and writing rules (hard rules)

Write like Preston talking to one person. It has to sound right read out loud.

**Language**

- Plain words, about a grade 5 reading level.
- Contractions are fine.
- Use complete, normal sentences.
- Say each thing once. Use less text than feels natural. When in doubt, cut.

**Banned patterns**

- Fragment stacks or punchy one-liners, like "You keep your job. Guests pay for the home."
- False-contrast pairs, like "Not X. Y.", "It's not X, it's Y" or "Same X, different Y."
- Self-interview questions, like "So why this? Because..."
- Staged reveals, like "The biggest piece is one thing: X."
- Slogan or aphorism endings, and fake-profound closing lines.
- Throat-clearing openers, like "Here's the thing" or "Let's be honest."
- Hype words: unlock, transform, game-changer, journey, empower, supercharge, secret, skyrocket, level up, and similar.
- Em dashes.
- Metaphors that need a second read.

**Headings and labels**

- Use sentence case for headings.
- Only the hero gets a short line above the headline. No other section gets a kicker or label above its heading.
- No subtitles or helper text under headings unless a slot asks for it.
- No labels that restate what is already on screen.

**Hedges and buttons**

- No hedges in the hero. Legal hedges go in the footer.
- Button labels are plain verbs, at most 4 words.

### 8B. VSL (about 2 minutes)

**Length.** 1:50 to 2:10. That is 290 to 330 spoken words, in 16 to 22 lines.

**Line format.** One line per row, exactly like this:

```
open.1 | [warm, matter-of-fact] Hey, it's Preston.
```

- The id is lowercase letters, a dot, and a number: `open.1`, `fit.2`, `course.1`, `ai.3`, `price.1`, `close.1`. The voice script parses this exact pattern.
- The cue is 2 to 5 words in square brackets at the start of each line. It sets the tone and is not read aloud (we tested this). Every sentence in the line uses that cue.
- Tone: warm, natural and conversational. Not flat, not dramatic. Cues that worked before:
  - [warm, matter-of-fact]
  - [gently, honest]
  - [calm, explaining]
  - [warm, a little excited]
  - [gently, walking through the numbers]
  - [warm, encouraging]
- Do not use loud, hyped cues. This is a sensitive moment for the reader.

**How the voice clone reads it (from a test on 31 October 2026)**

- Each sentence is generated on its own and joined with a short pause. So one sentence is one breath.
- Keep sentences under 20 words. Use a period where you want a pause.
- The clone speaks fast inside a sentence, about 280 words a minute. Short sentences help.
- Prices in digits read correctly: "$1,500", "$125", "12 months".
- "Splitit" is read as "split it", which is correct.
- Write "C P A" with spaces.
- Write "percent" instead of "%", "four oh one k" instead of "401(k)", and "H S A" instead of "HSA".
- Write "Preston AI" as is.
- Do not use em dashes, ellipses, parentheses, quote marks, or symbols like & in spoken lines.

**Beats, in this order**

1. **Open.** Preston thanks them for watching the workshop and for applying. 1 or 2 lines.
2. **The honest answer.** The full program isn't the right fit today, with the plain reason. No shame. 3 or 4 lines.
3. **What fits now: the course.** 2 or 3 lines.
4. **Preston AI.** What it is, plus 2 or 3 concrete things it does with their numbers. 4 to 6 lines.
5. **The honest limit.** It's an AI, not Preston, and not a licensed advisor. Final decisions go through their C P A. 1 or 2 lines.
6. **Price.** $1,500, or $125 a month for 12 months with Splitit. Full access from the first payment. If it would go on high-interest debt, wait. 2 or 3 lines.
7. **The door stays open.** They can apply for the full program again when things grow. 1 line.
8. **Call to action.** Click the button below the video. 1 or 2 lines.

**On-screen text.** For each line, give a short caption for the motion graphics, or "-" for none.

- At most 6 words per caption.
- Numbers and product names make the best captions.
- Write captions in normal spelling ("CPA", "401(k)"). The respelling rules apply to the spoken lines only.
